Five facts this template preserves

01

Timeline

What happened, when, and who became aware.

02

Stop authority

Who had authority to refuse or stop—and whether they could use it.

03

Control conditions

Which boundaries worked, failed, or were bypassed.

04

Evidence

Connect logs, decisions, communications, and observations.

05

Findings

Record unresolved issues and actions that reduce recurrence.

When to use it

  • After an AI-related incident or failure
  • After detecting a serious near miss
  • When stop authority or boundary controls are in doubt
  • Before stakeholder memories become fragmented

How to use it

  1. Choose the PDF or DOCX.
  2. Record the timeline and evidence before evaluating blame.
  3. Use accountable human review to determine findings and action.

What it does—and does not do

This template helps organize facts and decisions for review. It does not certify safety or compliance, provide a guarantee, or replace legal, security, technical, or accountable human judgment.
